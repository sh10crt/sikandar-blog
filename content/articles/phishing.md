Phishing is one of the most common forms of online deception.

## What is phishing?

Phishing attempts to trick people into revealing information or taking an action that benefits an attacker.

## How can you protect yourself?

Always check unexpected messages carefully before clicking links, opening attachments, or providing sensitive information.
